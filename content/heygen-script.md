# HeyGen intro video — script

Delivery: deadpan corporate for the first ten seconds, then drop the act and
warm right up. The joke only works if the opening is played completely straight.

Stage directions are in brackets — strip them before pasting into HeyGen.

---

## Main script (~85 seconds, ~215 words)

[Straight to camera. Deadly serious. Suit.]

Good afternoon. I'm here to talk to you about... **retirement planning.**

[Beat. Long one.]

[Break character. Smile.]

Alright, I can't keep that up. Hello Kirsten.

So. You asked me a very sensible question — how much should I be putting into my pension each month. And the honest answer is: nobody can tell you that until we know what you actually want your life to look like.

Because here's the thing the pension industry never asks. They'll talk about "moderate retirement outcomes."

They will not ask whether you still intend to be at a festival at sixty-eight, three days deep, telling a stranger this is the best set you've ever heard.

[Lean in.]

And you will be. We both know you will be.

That costs money. So does the wine. So does being the friend who says "shall we just get another bottle?" — which, for the record, is a beautiful quality and an expensive one.

So I built you this.

Move the sliders. Watch the number change. There's an assistant on the right that can see your figures and explain anything in plain English — ask it anything, it doesn't judge, and it never gets bored of the same question.

No jargon. No wrong answers. No selling you anything.

Let's work out what it costs to keep you dancing.

## Short cut (~35 seconds, ~90 words)

[Deadpan. Suit.]

Good afternoon. I'm here to talk to you about **retirement planning.**

[Beat.]

[Break. Smile.]

Hello Kirsten.

You asked how much to put in each month. Nobody can answer that until we know what you want your life to look like.

And I know what yours looks like. It looks like still being at a festival at sixty-eight, three days deep, insisting this is the best set you've ever heard.

That costs money. So does the wine.

So — move the sliders. Ask the assistant anything. Let's work out what it costs to keep you dancing.

---

## Notes

- **"Let's work out what it costs to keep you dancing"** is the line to keep
  whatever else changes. It's the whole product in eight words, and it reframes
  a pension from something dutiful into something she actually wants.
- The pause after "retirement planning" is doing real work. Give it a full
  second on the HeyGen timeline — the joke is the silence, not the words.
- Swap "sixty-eight" for whatever age lands best. It should be old enough to
  be funny and young enough to be plausible.
- If a specific festival or DJ would land better than the generic version, that
  will always be funnier than anything written by someone who wasn't there.
